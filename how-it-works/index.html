How it works

Care built around your day.

A Service Day starts with property approval. Residents receive the details and Parkline coordinates the agreed service and access.

For residents

  1. Get the invitation

    Your participating community shares the date and service options for its approved Service Day.

  2. Share the details

    Confirm your vehicle, usual space and any arrangements needed for safe exterior or interior access.

  3. We handle the care

    Parkline completes the agreed maintenance service and follows the communication plan for your property.
